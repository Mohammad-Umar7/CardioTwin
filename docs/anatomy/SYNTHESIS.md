# What is BodyParts3D, what is derived, what is synthesised

CardioTwin's 3D anatomy is built from **BodyParts3D** (one segmented body; CC BY-SA 2.1 JP). Where that source is
collapsed, sunk into the heart wall, mislabelled for our purpose or simply missing, the pipeline corrects or adds
geometry. Every correction is scripted, deterministic and listed here, with the literature value it follows
([`REFERENCE.md`](REFERENCE.md)). Nothing is sculpted by hand. The numbers quoted in the manifest's definitions are
filled in from the built geometry (`manifest.facts`), so a definition never contradicts the model
(`anatomy/tests/test_definitions.py`).

## Labels and attributes that exist only in CardioTwin

* `_SEGMENT` (coronary meshes): SCCT 2014 segment of the nearest labelled centreline point; the table with
  definitions and main-path / total branch lengths is `manifest.segments`, the per-point ranges are `vessels.json`
  `segments[].labels`. Present: 1–14 and 16. Absent by anatomy of this right-dominant heart: 15 (L-PDA), 17 (ramus),
  18 (L-PLB).
* `_VEIN` (cardiac veins) and `vessels.json` `veins`: labelled vein centrelines with their design radii (1 CS, 2 GCV,
  3 AIV, 4 MCV, 5 PVLV, 6 ACV, 7 LMV, 8 SCV, 9 RMV).
* `_RADIUS` (coronary meshes and `CardiacVeins`): lumen radius of the nearest labelled centreline point (scene units),
  so a viewer can inflate each vessel in proportion to its calibre and fade sub-pixel tips.
* `_TERRITORY` (heart walls): the LAD / LCX / RCA weights of `COLOR_0` under a name no glTF viewer multiplies into the
  albedo.
* `_DIST_HEART`, `_DIST_HILUM` (pulmonary artery and veins): geodesic distance from the cardiac end and signed
  distance from the lung hilum, so a viewer can keep the proximal vessels and fade the intrapulmonary tree.

## Known limitations that are not corrected yet

* The pulmonary valve ring is 31 mm wide and the tricuspid ring smaller than the mitral one (VLV-06: BodyParts3D's
  annuli); the SVC is 46 mm long (GV-11: in vivo 60–80 mm) and the pulmonary-vein ostia are 17 mm wide (GV-13).
* VLV-04 fits the tricuspid annulus to the valve vertices that touch the wall in the basal quartile of the valve: the
  septal hinge, lowered 22 mm, falls outside that quartile, so the check still reads the (basal) anterior and posterior
  hinge and reports −7 mm; the hinge lines themselves give the septal hinge 7 mm apical of the anterior mitral hinge.
* The coronary sinus opens in front of the IVC, 23 mm from the crux (COR-26 wants 15 mm), and between that ostium and
  the groove it rises up to 16 mm onto the posterior left-atrial wall (VEN-04: 58 % on the atrial side of the hinge,
  38 % parallel to it); CS + GCV measure ~157 mm on this large (37 mm) mitral annulus (VEN-09: ≤ 145 mm).
* Anterolaterally the GCV lies up to 19 mm from the mitral hinge (VEN-13: 15 mm), where the fused auricle overhangs the
  groove and the vein descends to the left-main bifurcation, itself 16 mm from the hinge ring.
* About 2 % of the vessel centreline samples still float 0.6–2 mm off the wall (VAS-01): acute marginal branches over the
  dips of the right-ventricular wall and a few stretches that cross a crease.
* The proximal LAD, circumflex and GCV pass under the tip of the left auricle through carved channels; channels
  that would cross the long-axis cut are not carved in that half.
